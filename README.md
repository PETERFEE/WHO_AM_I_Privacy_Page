# Privacy Policy — Who Am I? Attention Intelligence
Last updated: 16 August 2026

## Summary
This extension runs entirely on your device. It has no server, no account
system, and makes no network requests. Nothing it records is transmitted
anywhere, and the developer cannot see any of it.

## What is collected
Stored locally, in your browser only:
- The hostname of sites you view (e.g. "github.com") and how long you
  actively looked at each
- A topic category per site and per hour of the day
- A session timer
- Category corrections you make yourself
- A first-run flag

## What is read but not stored
To work out a page's subject, the extension reads the page title, breadcrumb
trail, OpenGraph/JSON-LD metadata, headings and up to 1200 characters of body
text. This is used for classification and discarded. A copy is held in browser
session storage for currently open tabs so a reading survives the browser
suspending the extension; it is deleted when you close your browser.

## What is never collected
- Full URLs, paths or query strings
- Passwords, credentials, payment details or account numbers
- Your name, email address or any identifier
- Your location
- The contents of emails or messages

## Sensitive sites
Banking, brokerage and medical sites are counted and categorised, because time
spent there reflects genuine interest. Their page content is never read — they
are recognised by hostname alone — their names are masked in the report, and
they are removed from any card you export or share.

These are never read and never recorded:
- Sign-in, password, checkout and payment pages, on every site including banks
- Password managers
- Email and private messaging
- Private file storage
- Adult and dating sites

## Data sharing
None. Data is not sold, transferred to third parties, used for any purpose
other than producing your report, or used for creditworthiness or lending.

## Retention and deletion
Data is pruned automatically on a retention schedule. "Reset" in the popup
erases everything, including the timer, immediately. Removing the extension
deletes all of its stored data.

## Changes
Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date, and in the
extension's store listing.
